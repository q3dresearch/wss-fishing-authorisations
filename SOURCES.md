## Each source in full

### `iccat.vessels.record`

**ICCAT — International Commission for the Conservation of Atlantic Tunas** (first party) · monthly · active

Three exports, ~11.3 MB per capture. Content-Type is application/vnd.ms-excel and the body is tab-separated CP1252 text -- it raises on byte 0xdc as UTF-8 -- which is why the gate matches on excel and the parser decodes cp1252 after trying UTF-8 strictly. ICCAT also misspells its own header: the inoperative export writes Op--Name where the others write OpName, and 436 of its 1,444 rows carry an operator. OwName/OpName and their addresses are filled on 48.4% of active rows and are a mix of companies (Rozafa shpk, INTERA COMPANY SA) and individuals (HARROLL WEAVER, J GREER). Raw goes to object storage complete; derived fingerprints individuals and names companies in clear -- see parsers/iccat_vessel_v1.py.

## Reusing this data

The captures in this repository are bytes as the publisher served them. Each
source's own licence or terms of service govern reuse — they are listed per
source above and they are not all the same. This repository's code is separate
from the data it collects; check the source before redistributing.
